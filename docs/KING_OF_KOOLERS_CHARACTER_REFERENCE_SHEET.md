### Core Dimensions & Proportions:
- **Outer Diameter**: Exactly **24.0 inches (Ø 24")**. Stout, wide circular cylinder with a **1:1.1 to 1:1.2 aspect ratio** on a single tier. It is wide and circular—never narrow, never oval, never square.
- **Wall Thickness**: **3.5 inches** of double-walled rotomolded UV-resistant polyethylene filled with continuous rigid R-8 polyurethane foam.
- **Modular Tier Heights**: **12-inch**, **18-inch**, **20-inch**, and **28-inch** stackable modules.
- **Maximum Stack Limit**: Up to **72 inches tall** (6.0 ft / 4-tier configuration).

## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Lid Design
- **Profile**: Flat, recessed circular lid sitting flush within the top interlocking collar rim.
- **Handle**: Single flush recessed center handle (integrated bar inside a center tray recess).
- **Options**:
  1. **Solar Generation Lid**: Integrated 10-Watt circular/rectangular monocrystalline solar panel with waterproof USB-A & USB-C ports.
  2. **Food-Grade Prep Disc**: Inlaid circular bamboo cutting board disc.
- **Seat Utility**: Flat top serves as an ergonomic outdoor camp seat rated for 500 lbs.

### B. Interlocking Collar & Camming Mechanism
- **Interlock**: 45° circumferential twist-lock bayonet camming lugs molded into the top rim of every tier.
- **Gasket Seals**: Dual-barrier hermetic system featuring a circumferential black EPDM compression lip seal plus a red food-grade silicone O-ring.
- **Latching**: Locks and seals airtight with a 45-degree rotational twist. **No exterior buckles, rubber T-latches, or draw latches.**

### C. Handles
- **Construction**: Heavy-duty rigid loop handles molded directly and seamlessly into the upper outer wall on opposing sides of every module.
- **Ergonomics**: Smooth cylindrical cross-section with subtle downward curve for comfortable dual-person two-handed lifting. Rated for 200 lbs per handle.

### D. Base & Drainage
- **Indexing Foot**: Recessed circular nesting foot on the bottom of each module that keys into the collar of the module below.
- **Drain Spigot / Water Bib**: Fast-flow quarter-turn food-grade drain spigot located strictly on the base of **20-inch and 28-inch tall modules**. Never located on 12-inch modules.

## 5. Master AI Prompting Directives

### Mandatory Rules (MUST INCLUDE):
1. **Wide 24" Cylinder**: "24-inch outer diameter wide stout circular rotomolded cylinder (aspect ratio 1:1.1)".
2. **Flat Flush Lid**: "Flat recessed circular lid sitting flush within top collar rim with flush center handle".
3. **Molded Loop Handles**: "Seamlessly molded-in heavy-duty side loop handles on opposing sides".
4. **Twist-Lock Seam**: "45-degree circumferential twist-lock bayonet cam seams between stacked modules".
5. **Rotomolded Matte Plastic**: "Matte UV-resistant rotomolded polyethylene with subtle micro-stippling".
