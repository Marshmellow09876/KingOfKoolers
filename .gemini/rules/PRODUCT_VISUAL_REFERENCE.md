# King of Koolers — Mandatory Product Visual Reference Rules

Whenever generating, modifying, rendering, or validating any image, 3D model, or visual asset for the King of Koolers:

1. **CONSULT THE MASTER REFERENCE SHEET FIRST**:
   - Master Reference Image: `assets/images/king_of_koolers_character_reference_sheet.jpg`
   - Master Reference Specification: `docs/KING_OF_KOOLERS_CHARACTER_REFERENCE_SHEET.md`

2. **MANDATORY GEOMETRIC RULES**:
   - Outer diameter is strictly **24.0 inches (Ø 24")**. Stout, wide circular cylinder (1:1.1 aspect ratio). Never narrow.
   - Lid is **flat, circular, and recessed flush** inside the upper interlocking collar. Single flush recessed center handle.
   - Handles are **heavy-duty rigid loops molded seamlessly** into opposing upper sides of each tier.
   - Inter-tier seams feature **45° twist-lock bayonet cam lugs** and dual EPDM/silicone compression gaskets. No external latches or buckles.
   - Drain spigot / water bib is located **strictly on the base of 20-inch and 28-inch modules**. Never on 12-inch modules.
   - Exterior shell is **rotomolded matte UV-polyethylene** with subtle micro-stippling.

3. **STRICT NEGATIVE PROMPTS (NEVER INCLUDE)**:
   - NO rectangular or square cooler bodies (Yeti Tundra/Igloo style).
   - NO curved, domed, or hinged lunchbox lids.
   - NO front latch buckles, metal draw latches, or rubber T-latches.
   - NO tall narrow water cooler / sports jug proportions.
   - NO wheels molded directly into the cooler body.
   - NO stainless steel / metal body (unless explicitly flagged for vacuum edition).
