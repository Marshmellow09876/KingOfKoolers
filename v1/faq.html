Why is the King of Koolers 24-inch cylindrical instead of rectangular?Thermal

A 24-inch cylinder eliminates 90-degree corner thermal bridges—where traditional chests lose cold fastest through thinned foam—and reduces external surface area by 12.8% for equivalent interior volume, cutting ambient heat gain by up to 34% for 10+ day ice retention.
