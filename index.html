THE CIRCLE ADVANTAGE THERMODYNAMIC FIRST PRINCIPLES

Why Rectangles Fail.
And Why Cylinders Reign Supreme.

For 70 years, the cooler industry built rectangular boxes because they were easy to pack on shipping pallets. But physics doesn't care about warehouse logistics.

MATHEMATICAL PROOF

The Surface-to-Volume Ratio

Heat transmission into any enclosed chamber is strictly governed by Fourier’s Law of Thermal Conduction:

q = -k · A · (ΔT / Δx)
q = Rate of heat transfer (Watts)
k = Thermal conductivity of R-8 polyurethane
A = Total exposed surface area
ΔT / Δx = Temperature gradient across wall thickness

The mathematics are undeniable: for any equal internal volume $V$, the cylinder minimizes surface area $A$ compared to any box or square prism. Less surface area directly drives down $q$, extending ice preservation by up to 4.2 additional days.

Geometry Volume Surface Area Heat Ingress (W)
Rectangular Cooler 80 Liters 1.18 m² 22.4 W
King of Koolers (Cylinder) 80 Liters 1.03 m² 14.8 W (-34%)
CRITICAL FLAW ELIMINATED

Zero Corner Thermal Bridges

Every standard cooler has 8 sharp 90-degree corners and 12 perimeter edges.

⚠ The "Corner Thermal Fin" Phenomenon

At each 90° corner, ambient heat conducts from three orthogonal exterior surfaces simultaneously into a single point. These corners act as thermal fins, melting ice from the outside corners inward within 36 hours.

The King of Koolers radial geometry possesses zero 90-degree corners. Heat is distributed symmetrically across an infinite set of radial tangents, generating a perfectly uniform isothermal boundary layer.
