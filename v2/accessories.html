Engineering & fitment notes

Standardized geometry across every 12″, 18″, 20″, and 28″ tier means your kit stays compatible as your stack grows.

01

Do accessories fit every 24-inch cooler height?

Yes. Every King of Koolers tier—12″, 18″, 20″, and 28″—shares the exact 24.0-inch outer diameter, 20-start 18° twist-lock collar, and concentric bottom nesting foot. Lid, divider, basket, and thermal isolation modules swap across every tier; drain-spigot attachments (#08 Draft Tap and #09 UV-C Core) mount exclusively on 20″ and 28″ base tiers.

02

What is included with 3D-printable maker files (#16–#31)?

Modules #16 through #31 are designed around support-free geometry segmented to fit standard 220×220mm FDM build plates. Each kit includes factory-ready specifications alongside downloadable multi-part .3MF and .STL files—including the 4-part circular base, dovetail wheel mounts, and roller feet for the #31 Wagon Dolly.

03

Are food-contact trays and marine cleats built to spec?

Every food-facing divider, deli carousel, burger stack, and hammock sling specifies BPA-free virgin food-grade PETG, Tritan, polycarbonate, or FDA platinum-cured silicone rated for commercial washdown. Exterior tie-down cleats and rod hardware use investment-cast 316 marine stainless steel and anodized 6061-T6 aluminum.

04

How do bundle discounts and stack reservations work?

Your kit persists automatically across sessions. Selecting any 2 accessories unlocks 10% bundle savings, 3 accessories unlocks 15%, and 4 or more accessories unlocks 20% off your outfitting total. Copy your plain-text build manifest anytime or proceed directly to tier selection on the reservation page.
