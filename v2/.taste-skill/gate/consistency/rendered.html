Thermodynamics

Rectangles lose cold through four exposed corners. A 24-inch cylinder drops external surface area by 12.8% and blocks thermal bridging with 3.5 inches of closed-cell R-8 foam.

How does the 18-degree cooler lock stay shut?

Twenty helical lugs clamp the 24-inch collar onto dual EPDM gaskets before the vertical deadbolt locks the joint.

Which cooler tiers include a drain spigot?

The quarter-turn drain spigot sits strictly on 20-inch and 28-inch bases. Upper tiers stay sealed.

Can the 12-inch top cooler keep pizza hot?

Yes. Continuous 3.5-inch R-8 walls hold hot dishes at 140 degrees above a 34-degree ice bunker.
